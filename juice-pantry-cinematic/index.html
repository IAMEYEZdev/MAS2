Our story

It Started With One Overgrown Garden

Est. in a kitchen, raised by a jungle

The Juice Pantry began the morning our courtyard garden stopped behaving — tomatoes in the gutters, kale through the kitchen window, a passionfruit vine that unlocked the back door. Rather than fight it, we bought a press.

Every bottle since has been a truce with the wild: heirloom produce from growers we know by first name, pressed within hours of picking, poured into glass, and never, ever heated. The jungle keeps growing. We keep bottling it.

— The Pantry Keepers
